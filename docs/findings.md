# Pilot findings: moving an investment account between Canadian firms

*Row IDs (P001-P059) match [`data/coded-posts.csv`](../data/coded-posts.csv). Post excerpts and the collector's notes are withheld from this repo; "Notes" below means those withheld notes.*

*Pilot, n=59 public posts. Self-initiated; public data; not affiliated with any institution. Review-site heavy, no Reddit, tags AI-suggested. Not a customer survey. Every verdict is provisional and the choice is made in docs/requirements.md.*

## Sample
- 59 posts (P001-P059): 27 forum, 31 review/complaint sites (53%), 1 app-store review, **Reddit 0**. One forum supplies 21 of the 27 forum rows.
- **Date flag:** The collector's coverage note says Feb 19, 2020 to Jul 23, 2026; the sheet includes P003 dated Jan 1, 2018. Nine rows predate 2023 (P003, P006-P008, P017, P037-P039, P041).
- Missing: Reddit (blocked; target about 70 rows); app stores; 29 of 88 verified rows dropped by the cap of 10 per firm, so no firm-by-firm comparison.
- Bias: review sites and complaint threads over-represent bad experiences; read every figure as "in these 59 public posts".

## Hypotheses
Frozen wording, scoring rule in [method](method.md), shares of n=59.
- **H1 confirmed, by one row.** Slow 15 + No status 3 = 18 (30.5%), against a 30% falsifier. Other ties Slow at 15 (does not outnumber it). One row fewer flips the verdict; so would the tagger's own alternate pain tag for any of P006, P011, P023, P034.
- **H2 unclear: not falsified on share and step (7 of 59, 11.9%, above 5%; all 7 at step 4), small n; cause unclear.** Notes and excerpts: 1 of 7 a data omission (P003), 3 firm-side (P043 fee-cash rule, P056 lost forms, P059 wrong addresses), 3 unstated (P013, P021, P029).
- **H3 wrong, small n.** Partial or wrong transfer: In-kind 0 of 8 (0%), Cash 0 of 4 (0%); not higher. All 3 Partial rows (P027, P046, P047) are kind Unknown, so weak evidence.
- **H4 confirmed, small n.** Fee surprise 6 vs Slow 15 (rarer); 6 of 6 at step 7. Its parenthetical (reimbursement caps, claim windows) fits 1 of 6 (P040).
- **H5 unclear: no data.** Non-registered rows: 0. Registered 29 (RRSP 12, TFSA 8, RESP 8, FHSA 1), Several 9, Unknown 21. Tax or registered-account worry: 0 rows.

## Candidate problems for the owner to choose from
Unranked; none is recommended.

**A. Slow processing (step 5)**
- Evidence: of H1's 18 rows, 13 plus P011 (lost forms) report elapsed time only: P005, P006, P009, P010, P011, P022, P025, P026, P028, P031, P051, P052, P055, P057 (8 still stuck, 6 completed). The top cell, Waiting x Slow (14, 23.7%), swaps P011 (step 4) for P023 (see B).
- Hurts: people mid-transfer; 3 of 8 RESP rows (P022, P025, P051, all still stuck).
- Needs: sending firm finishes its outgoing leg within a quoted time (Notes: sending 9 of the 14 (7 of 9 review-site; see (b)), receiving 1, P026); receiving firm chases and gives an expected date.
- Would NOT fix: missing updates (B), rejections, fees.
- Metric: share of transfers completed within the quoted time.

**B. No visible status or reachability (steps 4-5)**
- Evidence: the other 4 rows (6.8%): P018 (sending firm says no request received), P023 (20 calls), P034 (3 calls), P050 (no information after filing). 2 still stuck, 1 completed, 1 gave up.
- Hurts: people who cannot tell whether a transfer is moving.
- Needs: sending firm exposes outgoing-leg status and dates; receiving firm shows request state and escalates (Notes: P050 receiving, P018 between).
- Would NOT fix: processing time (A), rejections, fees.
- Metric: share of open transfers showing a current step and date.

**C. Rejections at the handoff (step 4)**
- Evidence: Rejected 7 (11.9%), all step 4. Outcomes: 4 still stuck (P021, P029, P056, P059), 1 gave up (P003), 2 unknown.
- Hurts: people who re-file repeatedly (P003, P029, P043, P056).
- Needs: receiving firm validates forms and fee cash before sending; sending firm returns a specific reason quickly.
- Would NOT fix: delays after acceptance, fee levels.
- Metric: share of requests accepted first time.

**D. Transfer-out fees (step 7)**
- Evidence: Fee surprise 6 (10.2%), all step 7; amounts $127, $150, $300 (P045, P049, P020); P040 reports refused reimbursement; fees also in P011, P041-P043. 4 of 6 completed.
- Hurts: people leaving, notably those without cash to pay (P011, P043).
- Needs: sending firm discloses the fee before confirmation; receiving firm states reimbursement terms.
- Would NOT fix: slowness, rejections, fee size.
- Metric: share of transfer-out requests where the fee is shown before confirming.

## What this pilot cannot tell you
- How common any problem is: no uneventful transfers as denominator.
- Anything about Reddit, app stores, or individual firms.
- Whether tags are right: all 59 AI-suggested, none individually re-checked by the author; a blind re-code of 20 rows by a second AI agent agreed with them on step 15/20 and on pain 19/20; H1 flips on one row.
- Cause or fault: no cause tag; transfer kind unknown in 46 rows, account type in 21, outcome in 12.
- Elapsed days: only 5 rows state days (P008, P012, P017, P020, P034; average 19.6 calendar days, mostly converted from weeks); the 14 rows in Exploratory (c) stating months have no day count, so the average understates waits; nine posts predate 2023, so processes may have changed.
